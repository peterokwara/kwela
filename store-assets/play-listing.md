## Short description

## Full description

Kwela helps you prepare for your next job application in one conversation.

Share a CV or job advert, then ask Kwela to help you:

- Build a CV from details you provide
- Tailor a CV to a specific role without adding experience you have not shared
- Draft a cover letter and answers to application questions
- Keep conversations and generated documents on your phone
- Share job adverts with Kwela from other Android apps

You stay in control. Review dates, employers, qualifications and every other detail before using a draft. Kwela does not find jobs automatically or send applications for you.

Kwela is designed for students, graduates and early-career job seekers. AI can make mistakes, so check every application before you send it. A draft does not guarantee an interview or a job.
