# Kwela demo video: Sheng on-screen script (draft 3)

Goes with [the storyline](demo-video-storyline.md). **No voiceover.** The story is told only by on-screen text in Sheng over the phone screen recording, like the 88.9K post (large subtitles). The lines use casual Sheng and English code-switching. “Wacha nitumie, Kwela” means “let me send it to you, girl”; the send-to-Kwela chat shot makes the wordplay clear. Change anything you would not actually say on camera.

## How we describe Kwela

Kwela is **CV writer wako**, kwa simu yako. Kenyans already know the cyber-café CV writer who charges 2K. We borrow that picture: Kwela is that writer, but she texts back, knows your CV, and is there when you apply.

- Say: *CV writer wako, mwandishi wako, anakuandikia, ameandika, anakutext.*
- Do not say: *AI, agent, bot, assistant, automation, "powered by".* Do not describe it the way Meta's Muse or other "AI agent" apps are described. People here do not hire an "agent"; they hire a writer.
- The writer does the writing. You still press submit (and on strict sites, you type the answers yourself).

## Where the jobs come from (real viewer habit)

Kenyans find jobs in WhatsApp groups and statuses, on poster photos, and in Instagram posts, not on job boards. The video should start there. This is the real "struggling moment": a job appears in a chat and you have nowhere to take it.

| Source | What the user does | Works in the app today? |
|---|---|---|
| WhatsApp message (text or link) | Long-press, Share, Kwela | **Yes.** The app accepts shared text and links. |
| Instagram post with a link or caption | Share, copy link, Kwela | **Yes** if shared as text or a link. |
| Poster photo (WhatsApp image, screenshot, Instagram image) | Attach the photo inside Kwela with the attach button | **Partly.** Photos attach in the chat (up to 5 MB). But sharing an image straight from WhatsApp or Instagram to Kwela does **not** work: the manifest accepts only `text/plain` shares. |

Text card ideas for this beat:
- **Job imedrop kwa WhatsApp group? Wacha nitumie, Kwela. 👀**
- **Poster ya job? Piga screenshot, attach kwa chat.** (only after the check below)

**Before filming the poster shot:** run a real poster photo through the app and confirm the reply reads the job correctly. A decision for you: add `image/*` to the Sharesheet so a photo can be shared straight from WhatsApp or Instagram? It is a small change in the manifest and the share handler, and it makes the best shot in the video possible.

## Lines to avoid

- "AI", "agent", "bot", "assistant", "powered by", or anything that sounds like a Meta/Muse-style launch.
- "30 bob" or any price that is not current. Answer "doo ngapi? / is it free?" in the pinned comment.
- "Utapata kazi" or "guaranteed". Say "CV, cover letter, majibu ready" only.
- "Anasubmit kwa niaba yako" until auto-submit is real.
- Anything that says Kwela gets past a site's no-bot check. The user types the answers.
