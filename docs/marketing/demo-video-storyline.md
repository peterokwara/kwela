# Kwela demo video: storyline (draft 1)

Framework: Jobs to Be Done (see [Moesta talk](jtbd-moesta-talk-transcript.md)). Language mirrors the CareerGo TikTok log: hooks, comments and the [7-day hook plan](../../../careergo-marketing/plans/2026-10-23%207-Day%20Hook%20Plan.md) (kept in `careergo-marketing`). Copy below is a draft in the same Swahili/English/Sheng mix viewers use. Edit freely.

> **Update (28 Oct):** The video has no voiceover; the story is on-screen Sheng text only. Kwela is positioned as **a personal CV writer** (like the cyber-café writer Kenyans already know), never as an "AI agent". The beat sheet below was written before this; use the Sheng script file for the current copy.

## 1. The job

> **When** I'm applying to many jobs and every site makes me retype the same things and write a new answer to each question, **help me** get the application 90% done for me, **so I** only have to submit my CV (or, on sites that ban bots, paste or type a few answers myself) and still get called for an interview.

Not "build a CV". CareerGo's CV tool already does that. Kwela's job is **finish the application**.

## 2. Forces of progress (from real viewer language)

| Force | What viewers actually say / do | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Push** (the old way fails) | Applied to 50 jobs, zero calls. "Mnaapply jobs lakini hamkui selected? HR anawaruka." CV doesn't match the job. Cyber charges 2K. | 7-day plan RJ/CY hooks; 94K and 88.9K posts |
| **Pull** (the new way) | "Nataka resume", "can you make me a good cv", "this cv got me a good job, best 60 bob I've ever spent". They want a result, not a tool. | 35.3K, 88.9K comments |
| **Anxiety** | "Doo ngapi?", "is it free?", "does careergo charge", "stop lying, am an HR officer…", "even after i paid 60 bob… my session has expired". 205K post: sites detect paste and tab switches, "HR anajua unatumia ChatGPT". | 22.9K, 88.9K, 205K posts |
| **Habit** | Cyber café, a friend with a laptop, copy-paste from ChatGPT, one generic CV for every job. | 88.9K comments ("Mimi yangu ya cyber…"), 205K post |

Real unmet asks in the 205K comments: "what about in forms", "answers za choices?". Viewers already want help with the application forms, not only the CV. This is the demand signal for Kwela.

## 3. Competing with (not other CV apps)

Cyber café (2K), a friend who "knows computers", ChatGPT copy-paste, or **not applying at all** (non-consumption). Position against these, never against other apps.

## 4. Choose what to suck at (honest promise)

Kwela gets you **90% there**:

- Tailors the CV and cover letter to the job.
- Drafts answers to the application questions.
- Where a site allows it, it submits for you. *(Roadmap. Check before filming.)*
- Where a site bans bots or flags paste, Kwela writes the answers and **you** type them in. That is exactly what the 205K post told people to do, so the video says it openly instead of hiding it.

We do not promise "a job". We promise "you won't spend 3 hours on one application".

> **Product check before filming:** the repo README today covers chat, tailored CV, cover letter and saved answers. Auto-submit is not in the app. Either film only what exists and tease submit as "coming", or build it first. Do not show a feature that is not real; commenters like the "stop lying" one will call it out.

## 5. Video plan

- **Length:** 35-45 s long cut (matches the winning 33-47 s posts). Plus a 10 s cut: hook, finished application, "link kwa bio".
- **Format:** 1080×1920, direct-to-camera hook (≤3 s), then phone screen recording of Kwela, large subtitles (same as the 88.9K post).
- **Tone:** Sheng/English, friend-to-friend, no corporate words.

### Beat sheet

| Time | Beat (JTBD step) | On screen | Line |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-3 s | **Struggling moment / first thought** | Face to camera, big text | "Umeapply jobs 50, zero calls? 😭 Shida si wewe. Ni vile unaapply." |

| 40-45 s | **CTA** | Finished application, Kwela icon | "Wewe unabonyeza tu submit. Link kwa bio." |

Recommended first test: **AI** (205K is the biggest post that is about application forms) with the **CR** reply cut as the second post.

### Things to avoid (from the comments)

- Do not say "30 bob" or any price unless it is current (viewers say they paid 60).
- Do not claim "ATS" without a visible ATS checklist ("That's not ATS CV").
- Do not show a payment or session screen that expires (a 22.9K commenter hit "session has expired" after paying).
- Answer "is it free? / doo ngapi?" inside the video or the pinned comment. It is the most repeated question.
